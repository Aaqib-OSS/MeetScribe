Welcome to MeetScribe

MeetScribe writes down your meetings while they happen, right on this PC. This assistant sets it up in a few steps.

  • 🔒Nothing leaves your computerNo account, no cloud, no API keys. Audio and text stay on this PC.
  • ⬇️One-time download of about 1 to 4 GBThe speech engine and the speech models. Takes about 5 to 20 minutes, depending on your internet.
  • 🎙️Two transcriptsA quick one live while you talk, then a more accurate one after the meeting.

Checking your PC

MeetScribe's speech engine runs on Python, a free programming tool. Python 3.11, 3.12 or 3.13 is needed.
